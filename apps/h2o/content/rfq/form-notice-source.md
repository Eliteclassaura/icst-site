# Quote request: collection notice

## Short notice (shown under the contact fields, above "Send request")

> We use these details to respond to your request, route it to the right ICS office and keep a record of it. Name, company, work email and country are required; everything else is optional. See our [Privacy Notice](/privacy-policy/).

## Expanded notice (behind a "How we use your details" link)

- **Why we ask:** to review your request, prepare a quotation, and send it to the right office (USA or Thailand). Our USA and Thailand offices reply within one business day.
- **What's required:** your name, company, work email, country, and the main question for your request type. Phone and the other details are optional; use **Skip** for any optional question.
- **Unfinished requests:** your answers stay in this browser tab until you select **Send request**. Nothing is sent to us before then. **Start over** clears them.
- **How you found us:** with your request, we record the page you came from, campaign tags in the link you followed, and any advertising click identifier the link carried.
- **How long we keep it:** 3 years after our last contact if you don't become a customer; for customers, for the relationship plus 7 years.
- **What not to include:** passwords, sensitive personal information, or anything unrelated to your project.
- **Questions:** privacy@h2ocooling.com. Our [Privacy Notice](/privacy-policy/) explains your rights, and our [Cookie Notice](/cookie-notice/) explains the storage we use.

## Rendering rules (for Main)

- The short notice sits directly above **Send request** in both the chat panel and the inline form.
- There is **no** pre-ticked box, and submitting does not sign anyone up for anything.
- This text replaces the RFQ string `privacy.note`.
