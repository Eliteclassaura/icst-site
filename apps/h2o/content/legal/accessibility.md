# Accessibility Statement

Industrial Cooling Solutions (ICS) wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use h2ocooling.com and request a quote.

## Our target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. This is our target, not a certification. We have not yet completed a full assessment that confirms the site meets it.

## Current status

We have reviewed the main pages and the quote form for keyboard use, screen-reader labels, colour contrast and mobile layout, and we are fixing the issues we found. Some parts of the site may not yet work as well as they should for everyone. If you run into a problem, please tell us (see below).

## Need help?

If any part of the site is hard to use, or you can't complete a quote request, email privacy@h2ocooling.com or call one of our offices:

- **USA:** +1-303-462-2000
- **Japan:** +81-90-4204-6742
- **Thailand:** +662-661-7723

Tell us which page you were on and what help you need. Please don't send sensitive personal information. We'll review your message and arrange a suitable alternative where we can.

## Review

We prepared this statement on 9 October 2026. We'll review it whenever we make significant changes to the site.
