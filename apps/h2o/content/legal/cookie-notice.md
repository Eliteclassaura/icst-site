# Cookie Notice

**Effective date:** 9 October 2026

This notice explains the cookies and browser storage h2ocooling.com uses. "Cookies" here also covers similar technologies such as browser session storage. The site is operated by Industrial Cooling Solutions (ICS).

**In short:** we use only the storage the site needs to run and to protect your quote request, plus analytics that do not use cookies. We do not use analytics cookies or advertising cookies.

## Storage that runs the site

This storage is needed to run the site and your quote request. It is always on.

| Name | What it does | Provider | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `h2o.rfq.draft.v1` (session storage) | Keeps an unfinished quote request in this browser tab so you can continue it. Nothing is sent to us until you select **Send request**. | ICS | Until you send the request, select **Start over**, or close the tab |
| Security cookies | Protect the site from automated attacks and abuse, and keep it available | Cloudflare | Short-lived; they expire when no longer needed |
| Sign-in and session cookies (staff areas only) | Keep ICS staff signed in to the private staff areas. Visitors to the public site do not receive them. | ICS | For the length of the session, or until the staff member signs out |

## Storage that records how you reached the site

| Name | What it does | Provider | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `h2o.rfq.attribution.v1` (session storage) | Records how you reached the site (campaign tags, landing page and referring page), so we can see which pages and links lead to quote requests | ICS | This browser tab. If you send a request, a copy is kept with it (see our [Privacy Notice](/privacy-policy/)) |

## Analytics without cookies

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits to the site and see which pages are used. It does not set cookies or use browser storage to follow you.

## Changes

We will update this notice before we start using analytics or advertising cookies.

## Your choices

You can block or delete cookies and browser storage in your browser settings. Some site features, such as continuing an unfinished quote request, may not work if you block necessary storage.
