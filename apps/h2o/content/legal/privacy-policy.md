## What we collect

**When you request a quote.** Our quote form and the chat-style request panel ask the same questions.

- **Required:** your name, company, work email, country, and what you need help with.
- **Optional:** your phone number and project details. Depending on your request, these include:
  - the part you need, with a model or serial number and quantity;
  - the tower type, number of cells and site location;
  - a description of the issue and your timing;
  - any other notes.

The required details let us review your request and route it to the right office. Without them, we can't complete an online request, but you can always call one of our offices instead.

**While you fill in the form.** If you leave a request unfinished, your answers are kept in this browser tab so you can pick up where you left off. They are not sent to us until you select **Send request**. **Start over** clears them.

**When you browse the site.** Our systems record technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, and security logs. With your request, we may also record:
- the page you came from and the page you first landed on;
- campaign tags in the link you followed (for example, UTM tags);
- an advertising click identifier, if your link carried one.

We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits to the site. It does not use cookies. See our [Cookie Notice](/cookie-notice/).

When you choose a translation language and your browser cannot translate on your device, the page text is sent to Google to translate it.

Please don't include sensitive personal information, passwords, or anything unrelated to your cooling project in your request.

- To review your request, ask for any missing details, and prepare a quotation.

- To keep the website and the request form secure and to prevent misuse.
- To understand how the site is used.
- To meet legal obligations, such as tax and warranty records, and to establish or defend legal claims.

**For visitors in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland:**
- We rely on our legitimate interest in answering business inquiries and running a secure website.
- We rely on steps you ask us to take before a contract, where you are the contracting party.
- We rely on legal obligations where a specific law applies.

## How long we keep it

| Information | How long |
|---|---|
| Quote requests that do not lead to a customer relationship | 3 years after our last contact with you |
| Customer records | For the length of the customer relationship plus 7 years (tax and warranty purposes) |
| Security and server logs | Only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting, then deleted or made anonymous |

When information reaches the end of its period, we delete it or make it anonymous. We keep information longer only where the law requires it or where it's needed for a legal claim.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:
- give you access to, or a copy of, your personal information;
- correct it;
- delete it;
- restrict how we use it;
- send it to you in a portable format.

You can also object to our use of it, and withdraw consent at any time; withdrawing doesn't affect use that was lawful before. The rights available to you depend on the law where you live.

To make a request, email privacy@h2ocooling.com. We may ask for information to confirm your identity. If we can't fully meet a request, we'll explain why.

**EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland.** You can complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

**United States.** You can ask us to:
- tell you what personal information we hold about you;
- correct it;
- delete it.

We won't treat you differently for making a privacy request.

**Thailand.** Under the Personal Data Protection Act, you may have rights to:
- access, correct and erase your data, and receive it in a portable form;
- object to its use and restrict it;
- withdraw consent.

You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee.

**Japan.** Under the Act on the Protection of Personal Information, you may ask us to disclose, correct, or stop using or providing personal data we hold, as the Act allows.
