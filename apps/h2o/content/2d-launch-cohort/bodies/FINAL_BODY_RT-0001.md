# Industrial Cooling Solutions

## What we do

Industrial Cooling Solutions works on large cooling towers: new tower construction, repair and maintenance of existing towers, thermal upgrades that recover lost capacity, and replacement parts.

## Cooling tower construction

Counterflow and crossflow towers in FRP, wood and concrete. Configuration, materials and cell count follow the duty and the site, so every enquiry is scoped before it is quoted.

## Repair, maintenance and thermal upgrade

Structural repair, planned maintenance and thermal upgrade work on towers already in service.

## Replacement parts

Fans, fan stacks, drive shafts, gearboxes, motors, fill media, drift eliminators, nozzles, FRP structural members and tower hardware. Part enquiries are quoted against your tower, not ordered from a list.

## Request a quote

Tell us what your tower is doing and what you need it to do, and the enquiry is scoped and quoted. The request form is the only conversion path on this estate.
