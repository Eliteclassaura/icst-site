# Cooling tower thermal upgrade

## What a thermal upgrade is for

A cooling tower thermal upgrade improves cooling tower efficiency without a full cooling tower replacement by optimizing airflow, water distribution, and heat transfer performance.

## Why cooling tower performance declines

Cooling tower performance declines due to aging system components, reduced airflow, fouled heat transfer surfaces, and inefficient water circulation in the cooling system.

In many industrial process environments, cooling systems interact with heat exchanger units, pumps, and refrigeration-based systems that include compressors, refrigerant, and thermostat controls. When these systems lose balance, the overall cooling effect and heat transfer efficiency drop significantly.

ICS evaluates the full system, including fans, water pumps, control systems, and operational technology, to identify performance loss and restore stable operation.

## Signs your cooling tower needs a thermal upgrade

- Rising heat load on connected process equipment
- Inconsistent output despite steady water flow
- Growing water usage with no matching gain in capacity
- Noticeable drift loss around the tower structure
- Corrosion or buildup on fan shafts, bearings, and other components

## What the upgrade changes

ICS upgrades key components, including fill media, fans, pumps, and nozzles, to lift tower efficiency and stabilize temperature control.

Where water conditions call for it, ICS adds a side stream filtration system to reduce dissolved solids and keep recirculating water cleaner for longer.
