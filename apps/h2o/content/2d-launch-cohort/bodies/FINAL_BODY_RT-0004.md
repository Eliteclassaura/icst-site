# About ICS

## Who we are

ICS was founded with the mission to create value for our customers by engineering, developing and implementing innovative industrial cooling solutions that are financially and technically feasible yet environmentally sustainable world wide.

## What we work on

New cooling tower construction, tower repair and maintenance, thermal and structural upgrade work, and replacement parts for towers of any make. Every enquiry is scoped and quoted individually.

## How we work

A key component in our philosophy is the overall quality of experience our customers realize when working with us. Design and performance are matched to the duty the tower has to meet, and the work is scoped before it is quoted.

## Request a quote

Tell us what your tower is doing and what you need it to do, and the enquiry is scoped and quoted. The request form is the only conversion path on this estate.
