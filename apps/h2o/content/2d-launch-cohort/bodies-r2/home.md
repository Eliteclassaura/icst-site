# Industrial Cooling Solutions

## What we do

ICS engineers, builds, repairs and re-rates large field-erected cooling towers, and supplies the replacement parts that keep them in service. Counterflow and crossflow, in FRP, wood and concrete, for the power, geothermal, refining, petrochemical, chemical, pulp and paper, steel and metals, and basic process industries.

Every tower is designed to a duty and a site rather than selected from a range, so every enquiry is scoped before it is quoted.

## New cooling tower construction

Counterflow and crossflow towers custom designed to your specification. Structure in FRP, wood or concrete; splash, film or hybrid fill selected against your water quality rather than assumed; induced or forced draft according to how corrosive the airstream is and where the mechanical equipment can safely sit.

Scope can run from the tower alone to the whole cooling block. ICS also supplies complete cooling tower systems including civil design, civil works and piling, pumps, piping, cable trays and other auxiliary equipment, and, where local licensing allows, can take the main contracting role for turnkey design, engineering, procurement, supply and installation.

## Repair, maintenance and thermal upgrade

Structural repair, mechanical work, planned maintenance and thermal upgrades on towers already in service, for all makes and brands of cooling tower, air-cooled condenser and air cooler.

Thermal inefficiency is a common cause of lost production in power plants, process plants and heavy industry. Some towers were under-designed from the start; others have lost capacity through age, fouling or damage. The first job is to determine the actual cause of the underperformance, not to sell a part. Where the cause is the fill, a wood-to-FRP or splash-to-film conversion may be the right answer; where it is water chemistry, the fill selection and the treatment programme are reviewed together.

Whether a tower is repaired, upgraded or rebuilt, the object is to bring its condition and thermal performance back as close to specification as the structure allows.
