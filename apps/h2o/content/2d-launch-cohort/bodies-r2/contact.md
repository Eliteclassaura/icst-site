# Request a quote

## How enquiries are handled

Tell us what your tower is doing and what you need it to do, and the enquiry is scoped and quoted. The request form is how every enquiry reaches ICS.

## What to send

The more we have up front, the fewer rounds it takes. Useful detail includes:

- What you need: cooling tower parts, a new or rebuilt cooling tower, or repair and maintenance work
- Which parts, if it is a parts enquiry: fan stacks, fans, gearboxes, motors, fill media, nozzles, FRP members, drift eliminators, drive shafts
- Whether the parts requirement is urgent
- The make, model, cell arrangement and duty of the tower
- Any documentation you hold for it — drawings, data sheets, previous inspection reports

For drive shafts, fans and gear boxes there are enquiry data sheets, and for a new tower there is a data sheet, listed with the [cooling tower resources](/resources/). Completing the right one for your project and attaching it to the enquiry is the fastest route to an accurate answer.
