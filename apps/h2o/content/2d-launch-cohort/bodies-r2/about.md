# About ICS

## Who we are

ICS is made up of people from the cooling tower industries, working across the whole life of a tower: new construction, planned maintenance, unplanned repair, thermal and structural upgrade, and replacement parts.

## How we work

A cooling tower is not a catalogue purchase. The duty, the water, the site and the shutdown window decide what can be built or repaired, and in what order, so every job starts by establishing those rather than by proposing a product.

That applies to the small work as much as the large. A fill change and a new tower are the same question asked at different scale: what is limiting this tower, and what is the least disruptive way to fix it.

## Industries we work in

Power, geothermal, refining, petrochemical, chemical, pulp and paper, steel and metals, and basic process industries.

## What we do

New tower construction. Tower repair, reconstruction and maintenance. Cooling tower thermal and structural upgrades. Replacement parts across the common makes and configurations.

Technical support covers the whole of that scope: project engineering, procurement, field support and after-sales technical assistance.

## How to reach us

Every enquiry on this site goes through the request form. Tell us what your tower is doing and what you need it to do, and include any documentation you hold for it.
