# Cooling tower thermal upgrade

## What a thermal upgrade is for

A thermal upgrade recovers cooling capacity from a tower that is already in place, by correcting what is actually limiting it — airflow, water distribution, or heat transfer surface — rather than replacing the tower.

## Why performance declines

Thermal inefficiency is a common cause of lost production in power plants, process plants and other heavy industrial plants. Some cooling towers were under-designed from the beginning. Others have become inefficient through age, fouling or damage.

Fouled fill is usually the result of the fill that was chosen and the treated water being incompatible with the site's operating conditions. Sometimes the water quality itself has worsened and the fill type, the filtration or the treatment programme needs to be reassessed. Diagnosis comes before specification: ICS reviews the operation to isolate the cause of the fouling or the loss.

Aging components contribute as well — reduced airflow from fans and drives past their service life, degraded water distribution through worn or clogged nozzles, and fouled heat transfer surfaces.

## What we determine first

ICS's thermal upgrade specialists work with you to determine the actual cause of the underperformance and to find the solutions that work for your project, budget and schedule.

The measured outcome is cold water temperature: that is what a thermal upgrade moves, and it is what lets the plant recover capacity. Before the work is specified, ICS will say what cold water improvement it expects to be achievable on your tower, and on what operating data that expectation rests.

Every upgrade is built around the site's real operating data, water balance and mechanical condition, not a generic spec sheet.

## What an upgrade can include

- Fill media, nozzle and fan blade replacement
- Inspection and replacement of drift eliminators past their service life
- Basin cleaning to control biological growth before it spreads
- Replacement of bearings, gearboxes and motors that no longer match current operating conditions
- Controls tuning against real cooling demand rather than nameplate ratings
- Side stream filtration, where water conditions need to be brought within what the fill can tolerate

## Conversions

**Wood to FRP.** Converting a wooden tower structure to fibreglass-reinforced plastic, assessed against the age and condition of the tower as well as budget and future requirements. A wood-to-FRP fan deck conversion is a separate, smaller option where reliability and access safety are the concern.

**Splash to film fill.** Converting a splash-fill tower to a film-fill system, where water quality supports it, to raise thermal performance and reduce fan power. The film fill type is selected with your team against the water you actually have.

## Structural upgrade

Structural work runs alongside thermal work: casing, fan decks, louvres, basin and the tower structure itself. A tower that cannot be safely accessed cannot be reliably maintained, so access and deck condition are part of the same assessment.
