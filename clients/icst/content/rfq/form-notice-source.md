## Expanded notice (behind a "How we use your details" link)

- **Why we ask:** to review your request, prepare a quotation, and send it to the right office.
- **What's required:** your name, company, work email, country, and the main question for your request type. Phone and the other details are optional; use **Skip** for any optional question.
- **Unfinished requests:** your answers stay in this browser tab until you select **Send request**. Nothing is sent to us before then. **Start over** clears them.
- **What not to include:** passwords, sensitive personal information, or anything unrelated to your project.
- **Questions and your rights:** see our [Privacy Notice](/privacy-policy/). Our [Cookie Notice](/cookie-notice/) explains the storage we use.
