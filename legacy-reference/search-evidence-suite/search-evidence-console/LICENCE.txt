THE LICENCE MARKER, PLAINLY
---------------------------
Enter your key from the sidebar. An unlicensed copy shows a marker there.

The check runs in your browser, on your machine. It is not enforcement and
is not presented as any: anyone who wants to remove it can, and no feature
is gated behind it. What it does is make an unlicensed deployment visible
to the staff and clients who open the page, which is what matters to a
business that could be asked about its software licensing.

This is how licensed browser software works generally -- AG Grid and Kendo
UI both ship licensed JavaScript that runs entirely in the customer's
browser, and neither blocks; an invalid key shows a watermark and warns in
the console. The product keeps working.

The key carries two check characters so a typo is caught and reported as a
typo. That is deliberately not cryptographic and is not claimed to be.
